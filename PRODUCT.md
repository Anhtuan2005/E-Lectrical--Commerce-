# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vietnamese customers comparing and buying consumer technology, and shop staff managing products, inventory, orders, promotions, shipping, and after-sales requests. Customers should be able to complete routine shopping tasks quickly on desktop and mobile.

## Product Purpose

Techvora is a portfolio e-commerce application that demonstrates a complete, credible purchase lifecycle: product discovery, cart and direct checkout, vouchers, payment, fulfillment, account management, and after-sales support. Success means each workflow behaves predictably, preserves transaction integrity, and is easy to explain in a technical interview.

## Brand Personality

Clear, capable, and calm. Copy is concise Vietnamese, actions feel familiar, and technical sophistication stays behind the interface.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketplace clutter, decorative effects that compete with product information, ambiguous purchase actions, inconsistent controls, and layouts that trade mobile usability for visual novelty.

## Design Principles

- Make the next shopping action obvious and keep its effect unsurprising.
- Show prices, stock, delivery, and status information where decisions happen.
- Use consistent controls and feedback across storefront, checkout, and admin workflows.
- Keep common tasks fast on small screens without hiding required information.
- Let reliability and complete workflows carry the portfolio story.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible focus states, readable contrast, keyboard-operable flows, polite live feedback, and reduced-motion support. Vietnamese labels should remain clear without relying on color alone.
